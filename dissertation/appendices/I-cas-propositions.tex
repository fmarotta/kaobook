\chapter{CAS Propositions}
\labch{appendix-cas-propositions}

This appendix summarizes the fifteen Complex Adaptive Systems propositions
introduced in Chapter~\ref{ch:cas}. Full visualizations of each proposition
are available in the online supplementary materials.

\tableminimal
\begin{center}
\small
\begin{tabular}{@{}clp{8cm}@{}}
\toprule
\textbf{\#} & \textbf{Mechanism} & \textbf{Proposition} \\
\midrule
\multicolumn{3}{l}{\textit{Emergence (P1--P3)}} \\[0.3em]
P1 & Emergence & Role multiplicity is the norm, not the exception. \\
P2 & Emergence & Constellation-level functions exceed the sum of individual role performances. \\
P3 & Emergence & Role emergence accelerates with interaction density. \\[0.3em]
\multicolumn{3}{l}{\textit{Self-Organization (P4--P6)}} \\[0.3em]
P4 & Self-org. & Role differentiation emerges without central coordination. \\
P5 & Self-org. & Hierarchy emerges from accumulated advantage, not formal assignment. \\
P6 & Self-org. & Niche formation reduces direct role competition. \\[0.3em]
\multicolumn{3}{l}{\textit{Co-Evolution (P7--P9)}} \\[0.3em]
P7 & Co-evolution & Constellation structure and policy environment co-evolve. \\
P8 & Co-evolution & Success of one intermediary alters the fitness landscape for others. \\
P9 & Co-evolution & Constellations co-evolve with technological trajectories. \\[0.3em]
\multicolumn{3}{l}{\textit{Non-Linearity (P10--P12)}} \\[0.3em]
P10 & Non-linearity & Constellation change follows punctuated equilibrium patterns. \\
P11 & Non-linearity & Small interventions can trigger disproportionate restructuring. \\
P12 & Non-linearity & Path dependence constrains role repertoires. \\[0.3em]
\multicolumn{3}{l}{\textit{Feedback Loops (P13--P15)}} \\[0.3em]
P13 & Feedback & Reinforcing feedback produces role concentration. \\
P14 & Feedback & Balancing feedback maintains role diversity. \\
P15 & Feedback & Fitness requires multi-dimensional adaptation. \\
\bottomrule
\end{tabular}
